A Fresh Chapter for FENDI’s Peekaboo

Soft, lightweight, and endlessly versatile, the Peekaboo Soft Small is FENDI’s newest addition to the iconic line.

January 15, 2025

Introduced during the Women’s Spring/Summer 2025 runway and offering a modern take on a well-known classic, the Peekaboo Soft Small combines a relaxed silhouette with refined craftsmanship. This new addition is a testament to the house’s mastery of blending style with purpose.

To kick off the launch, FENDI teamed up with Emir Eralp to create a campaign video set to Little Dragon’s dreamy track “Little Man.” The blend of fashion, music, and art shows off the brand’s creativity just as the Italian house gears up for its centenary celebrations.

Crafted from lightweight calf leather, the Peekaboo Soft Small keeps the spirit of the original Peekaboo alive while adding an updated, adjustable strap. Whether carried by hand, worn over the shoulder, or styled crossbody, it maintains the core aesthetic Silvia Venturini Fendi is known for: timeless yet effortlessly wearable.

If you’re into classic colors, look out for black or dove. For a bolder option, there’s a bright green that really stands out, plus a fringe-adorned version capturing the energetic mood of the SS25 collection. And for anyone looking to take it up a notch, the croco dégradé editions deliver a touch of elevated luxury.

Ready for your next statement piece? Don’t miss out on this fresh spin on a legendary style. The Peekaboo Soft Small is now available at select FENDI boutiques and at fendi.com.
