Tom Papa and Nambé Rise to the Occasion With Their Popular ‘Breaking Bread’ Collection

Written by Jason Price on September 1, 2026

Bread making is having a moment—and Nambé is inviting everyone to get in on it.

Since launching in September 2025, Nambé’s Tom Papa Breaking Bread Collection has quickly become one of the brand’s most popular new introductions. Created in collaboration with Tom Papa—the acclaimed comedian, actor, Netflix star, SiriusXM host and longtime bread-baking enthusiast—the collection has connected with consumers through its distinctive combination of personality, functionality and Nambé’s signature modern design.

The collection’s success has been fueled by the continued popularity of sourdough, homemade bread and from-scratch baking across social media. Designed for bakers of every experience level, the assortment brings together the essential tools needed to make, shape, score, slice and serve bread beautifully—from first-time bakers creating their first loaf to experienced enthusiasts perfecting their sourdough starter.

Since its introduction, the Tom Papa Breaking Bread Collection has developed a strong following among bread enthusiasts and has also emerged as a distinctive gifting choice for bridal, wedding, housewarming and other milestone occasions. Its thoughtful functionality, premium materials and contemporary design give the collection broad appeal extending well beyond the dedicated home baker.

“Bread making is one of those things that can be as simple or as obsessive as you want it to be,” says Tom Papa. “You can make a loaf with almost nothing, but having the right tools makes the process more enjoyable—and a lot more satisfying.”

Designed by Nambé in partnership with Tom Papa, the Tom Papa Breaking Bread Collection combines Nambé’s renowned approach to modern design with Tom’s genuine passion for bread making. Stainless steel, acacia wood, natural cane and white stoneware come together in a cohesive assortment that is as beautiful displayed on the countertop as it is practical throughout the baking process.

Tools for Every Step of the Bake
The Breaking Bread Whisk is designed for mixing starters, doughs and batters, while the Breaking Bread Bench Scraper helps bakers divide and shape dough and keep the work surface clean. Together, the two pieces are also available as the Breaking Bread Gift Set, making an approachable entry point for anyone beginning their bread-making journey.

For sourdough bakers, the Breaking Bread Lame offers precision when scoring proofed dough before baking, complete with an acacia wood handle, magnetic safety cap and five stainless steel razor blades. The Breaking Bread Proofing Basket is crafted from natural cane to support even dough rising while its rattan coils help create the signature spiral associated with artisan loaves.

Once the loaf comes out of the oven, the Breaking Bread Serrated Knife is designed to slice through a crisp crust without crushing the soft interior. Its acacia wood handle provides a comfortable grip and balanced feel, making it a natural everyday bread knife for everything from sourdough to sandwich loaves.

And for the final reveal, the Breaking Bread Board combines an acacia wood cutting surface with a matching crumb tray, making it easy to slice, serve and enjoy fresh bread while keeping countertops tidy. The Breaking Bread Cutting Board & Knife Set brings both pieces together for a complete bread-slicing and serving solution.

The collection also extends beyond the bake itself, with Breaking Bread Canisters designed to organize flour, sugar and other baking essentials, as well as measuring spoons, wood utensils and other kitchen accessories that make bread making feel like part of the everyday kitchen.

From Sourdough Trend to Everyday Ritual
The current resurgence in sourdough and artisan baking is about more than the finished loaf. Bread making has become a creative, hands-on ritual—one that combines craft, patience and the satisfaction of making something from scratch. Social media has helped fuel that enthusiasm, turning everything from feeding a sourdough starter to scoring a loaf into highly shareable moments.

The Tom Papa Breaking Bread Collection taps into that movement while making the category accessible beyond the dedicated sourdough community. Its growing popularity reflects the broader appeal of bread making as a hobby, a form of creative expression and a way to bring people together.

These are tools for the person baking their first loaf, the home cook who wants to make weekend bread, the experienced sourdough baker refining their technique or the gift-giver looking for something useful, unexpected and beautifully designed.

That versatility is part of what is making the collection increasingly desirable: it speaks to the passionate baker, but it also works as a thoughtful gift for newlyweds, newly engaged couples, new homeowners, hosts and anyone who loves a beautiful kitchen.

Because bread making doesn’t have to be intimidating—and you don’t have to be an expert to have the right tools.

Bread Making, Beautifully Designed
At the heart of the collaboration is a simple idea: the tools you use to make something should be as thoughtfully designed as the thing you’re making.

The Tom Papa Breaking Bread Collection brings Nambé’s distinctive design language into the world of bread making, creating a collection that works hard in the kitchen while looking right at home in a modern space.

What began as a collaboration inspired by Tom Papa’s love of bread is becoming something bigger: a collection that brings together the passion of today’s bread-making movement with the timeless appeal of beautifully designed kitchen tools.

For anyone inspired by the sourdough boom, curious about baking from scratch or simply looking for better bread-making tools, the message is simple:

Make the loaf. Enjoy the ritual. Break bread.

Explore the complete Tom Papa Breaking Bread Collection at Nambé.com or contact our sales team at wholesale@portmeirion-usa.com.

About Tom Papa—Tom Papa is a celebrated comedian, actor, writer, and podcast host, known for his relatable humor and love of bread. He hosts the SiriusXM show Come to Papa, the podcast Breaking Bread with TomPapa, and has multiple Netflix comedy specials. Beyond the stage, Tom is a passionate home baker, often sharing his love of sourdough with fans around the world.

About Nambé—Founded in 1951 in Santa Fe, New Mexico, Nambé is renowned for its timeless designs that bring style and function to the modern home. From serveware to décor, each piece is crafted with a dedication to artistry, quality, and innovation. A part of the Portmeirion Group family of brands, Nambé products are found in homes, retailers, and museum collections worldwide.
